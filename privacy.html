Welcome to our mobile application ("the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you use our App on iOS or Android devices.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information to provide and improve our services:

2.1 Name and Contact Information

We may collect your name and email address when you register an account or contact us for support. This information is used to identify your account, communicate with you, and provide customer support.

2.2 Location Data

With your permission, we collect precise or approximate location data from your device. Location data is used for game features that rely on your geographic position (such as location-based gameplay, maps, or regional events). You may disable location access at any time through your device settings; however, certain features of the App may not function properly without it.

2.3 Usage and Analytics Data

We automatically collect certain usage data when you interact with the App, including:

  • Gameplay activity, progress, scores, and session duration
  • In-app actions, feature usage, and navigation patterns

This data helps us understand how players interact with the App, diagnose technical issues, and improve game performance and features.

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide technical support

  • To comply with applicable legal obligations

We store your data on secure servers and implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the App's services. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal data within a reasonable timeframe, except where we are required to retain it for legal or compliance purposes.
