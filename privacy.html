LingoFire — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 23, 2026 · Last Updated: May 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to LingoFire ("the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and safeguard your information when you use the App on Android or iOS devices.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information to provide and improve the App:

2.1 Google Account Information (Sign-In)

LingoFire requires you to sign in with a Google account to use the App. When you sign in, we receive the following information from Google:

  • Your email address
  • Your Google display name
  • Your Google profile photo (if you have one)
  • A unique Google user identifier

This information is used solely to create and authenticate your account, identify you within the App, and provide customer support. We do not access any other Google services or data on your behalf, and we do not post anything to your Google account.

2.2 Game Activity and User-Generated Content

When you play LingoFire, we collect the following game-related data:

  • Display name you choose for the App
  • Answers you submit during rounds (words you type for the Name, Animal, Place, Thing categories)
  • Scores, round results, and game history
  • Room codes you create or join
  • Purchase records if you buy the optional ad-free / premium upgrade (see Section 4)

This data is necessary to run multiplayer games and show results. Note that your display name, answers, and scores are visible to the other players in the same game room.

2.3 Device, Diagnostic, and Analytics Data

We automatically collect technical information when you use the App, including:

  • Device type, operating system version, and App version
  • Crash reports, error logs, and performance traces
  • Advertising identifiers used by Google AdMob to display ads (see Section 5)
  • General usage patterns such as features used and session duration

This data helps us diagnose technical issues, improve performance and stability, and understand how the App is used.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To create, manage, and authenticate your account via Google Sign-In
  • To run multiplayer games, match you with other players, and display game results
  • To submit your answers to an automated scoring service (see Section 4) so the App can determine which answers are valid for each round
  • To display advertisements via Google AdMob and to operate basic ad measurement
  • To analyse usage patterns, diagnose crashes, and improve App performance and content
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide technical support
  • To detect and prevent fraud, cheating, abuse, and security threats
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4.1 Service Providers We Use

LingoFire is built on services provided by Google. The following Google services receive and process data on our behalf:

  • Google Sign-In — authenticates you when you log in. Google receives standard sign-in metadata (your IP address, device info). See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Authentication — manages your account credentials and session.
  • Cloud Firestore — stores your display name, game history, room data, answers, and scores so games can be played in real time across devices.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — collects crash reports and error logs so we can fix bugs.
  • Firebase Analytics — collects anonymous usage events (such as game completions and session length) and standard device/region metadata so we can understand engagement and improve the App.
  • Firebase Cloud Functions — runs server-side logic for the App, including the answer-scoring function.
  • Google Gemini API — when a round ends, the words you submitted as answers are sent (without your name or email) to Google's Gemini language model via our Cloud Function so it can determine which answers are valid words in the relevant category. See Google Gemini API Terms.
  • Google AdMob — displays advertisements in the App and may collect your advertising identifier and other device signals to serve ads. See Section 5 and Google Ads Privacy.
  • Google Play Billing — processes optional in-app purchases (such as the ad-free / premium upgrade). Google handles your payment; our Cloud Function receives only a purchase token and product identifier (never your card details) to verify the purchase and unlock the feature on your account.

These providers are bound by their own privacy policies and our service agreements with them, and they process data only as needed to provide their services.

4.2 Other Players

When you play a multiplayer game, the other players in your room can see your display name, the answers you submit, and your scores. Please choose a display name you are comfortable sharing with strangers.

  • Legal compliance: If required to do so by law, regulation, or valid legal process (such as a court order or government request).
  • Protection of rights: To enforce our Terms of Service, protect the safety of our users, or prevent fraud or illegal activity.
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the successor entity. We will provide notice before your personal data is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

5. Advertising

LingoFire displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier and certain device signals to deliver ads and to measure ad performance.

You can control ad personalisation in your device settings:

  • Android: open Settings → Google → Ads to reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised advertising.
  • iOS: when prompted by the App Tracking Transparency dialog, you can choose to disallow tracking. You can also manage this at any time under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised based on your activity across other apps and sites.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure operated by our service providers. We rely on industry-standard technical and organisational measures, including encryption of data in transit, access controls, and the security features provided by Google Cloud and Firebase, to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App's services. If you request deletion of your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal data within a reasonable timeframe, except where we are required to retain it for legal or compliance purposes. Crash reports and aggregated analytics may be retained for diagnostic purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

LingoFire is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum digital age in your jurisdiction). Google Sign-In requires a Google account, which Google's terms generally restrict to users above this age. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under the applicable minimum age without verifiable parental consent, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information without your consent, please contact us immediately at the address provided in Section 11.

Where required by applicable law, we and our service providers implement appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) to protect your data during international transfers.
